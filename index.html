Academic Project Overview

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) & AI Framework

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) is an international pedagogical mechanism that formally assesses competencies acquired outside of traditional classrooms. Whether gained through commercial freelance tenure, self-directed open-source contributions, or enterprise on-the-job training, RPL provides an equitable pathway for individuals to convert real-world experience into official qualifications.

1. How does this AI prototype evaluate prior learning?

The diagnostic engine implements a 4-pillar multi-criteria evaluation rubric:
• Domain Benchmark Matching (35%): Evaluates self-reported competencies against standardized industry matrices (e.g. Frontend, Backend, CI/CD, SQL).
• Practical Narrative & Evidence Parsing (30%): Analyzes project descriptions for domain-specific technical vocabulary, quantifiable achievements, and verifiable portfolio URLs.
• Tenure & Engagement Continuity (20%): Considers total years in industry, employment modality (freelance vs. full-time), and role depth.
• Foundational & Non-Formal Credentials (15%): Rewards online courses, bootcamp certificates, and self-directed study.

2. How are the 4 RPL Qualification Levels defined?

• Level 4 (85-100 pts) - Advanced Mastery: Candidate demonstrates end-to-end industry competence with robust artifacts; fast-track full RPL credit recommended.
• Level 3 (68-84 pts) - Practitioner Competence: Solid practical foundation with standard qualification eligibility; portfolio interview recommended.
• Level 2 (45-67 pts) - Developing Competence: Partial RPL qualification with targeted bridging modules required for deficient competencies.
• Level 1 (0-44 pts) - Foundational Awareness: Early-stage skill set; comprehensive preparatory coursework advised before formal assessment.

3. Important Prototype & Academic Disclaimer

This project was created for educational coursework demonstrating human-in-the-loop AI assistance for educational admissions and vocational audits. Scores, diagnostics, and bridging recommendations are algorithmic simulations intended to guide candidate self-preparation. In formal accredited institutions, final RPL awards are always determined by authorized human assessors and academic boards.

4. Technology Stack & Deployment Architecture

Built with modern, beginner-friendly Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and ES6 JavaScript with zero external backend dependencies. Charting is powered by Chart.js (CDN). All assessment records are stored in browser localStorage. Designed for instant deployment to GitHub Pages or any static web host.
